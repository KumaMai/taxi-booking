# Editorial Travel Design Brief

## Subject and job

Time Taxi Khaolak serves international travelers moving between Phuket Airport,
Khao Lak, and Southern Thailand. The public site's single job is to turn travel
intent into a confident booking enquiry.

## Token direction

| Role | Token |
|---|---|
| Deep Sea | `#092C3C` |
| Ink | `#13232B` |
| Sand | `#F4EBDD` |

| Route Gold | `#C89B3C` |
| Foam | `#FAF9F5` |

## Type

- Fraunces: restrained editorial headlines and route statements.
- DM Sans: navigation, forms, prices, tables, and body copy.
- Use sentence case for actions and plain traveler vocabulary.

## Signature

An annotated route ribbon connecting **Phuket Airport ↔ Khao Lak**. It can appear
as a hairline map-like rule, an arrival stamp, or a compact route card, but it
should remain one system rather than scattered decoration.

## Priority order

1. Global shell and design tokens
2. Home / Landing
3. Progressive Booking
4. Price List
5. Contact
6. Reviews
7. About
8. FAQ
9. Travel Recommendations
10. Admin shell, dashboard, bookings, prices, reviews, FAQ, settings

## Booking wireframe

```text
Step 1 Trip ─── Step 2 Contact ─── Step 3 Review
┌──────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────┐
│ current step fields          │ route/vehicle summary  │
│ validation near the field    │ reassurance + contact  │
│ [Back]                 [Next]│ price link / help      │
└──────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────┘
```

## Critique guardrails

- Do not use a generic full-screen gradient hero.
- Do not let editorial styling reduce form legibility or keyboard focus.
- Spend visual boldness on the route ribbon; keep controls quiet and obvious.
- Respect reduced motion and preserve readable contrast on Sand and Deep Sea.
